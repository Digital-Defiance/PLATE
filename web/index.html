What is an SVG, and what can I edit?

An SVG is a vector image made of shapes and paths. You can resize it without pixelation and edit its parts in a vector editor. PLATE works best on flat artwork. Photos and very small details can need cleanup. Lettering becomes paths, not editable font text, and objects that share a color may remain joined.
